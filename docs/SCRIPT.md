# The Director's Cut: the script (from Marwan, Nov 15 13:00 Cairo)

The game follows this script. How it maps onto what is already built is in `docs/SCRIPT_ADAPT.md`.

## The visual rule: "The Director's Cut"
- **THE LEAD (Vance): The Final Cut.** Always rich 35mm anamorphic widescreen (letterboxed 2.39:1), warm film grain,
  dramatic lighting, sweeping cinematic camera angles. To Vance he is the star of a smooth, stylish 1970s thriller.
- **THE CREW (The Uncut Feed):** cutting to the other three removes the letterbox bars and the grain. Their screens
  are crisp, raw, unpolished, grounded: the gritty, stressful reality that makes Vance's "movie" look effortless.
- **THE CUT:** every character switch is a sharp film-splice cut, with the clack of a clapperboard or the whir of a
  projector reel.

## The cast
1. **VANCE** (the star / infiltrator): suave, unflappable, lives inside his own movie. Screen: 35mm cinema filter.
2. **MAX** (the tech / van): sweaty, fast-talking, tangled cables and CRT monitors across the street. Screen: raw,
   cramped interior.
3. **NORA** (the eye / catwalk rigger): agile, cool-headed, in the theater's steel lighting rafters with a bird's-eye
   view of the floor. Screen: raw, high-altitude vertigo.
4. **COLE** (the muscle / backstage): heavy-hitting, pragmatic, the dark corridors behind the velvet curtains.
   Screen: raw, claustrophobic, gritty.
5. **ARTHUR PENDLETON** (the villain): eccentric, ruthless billionaire collector who bought the stolen master reel of
   *Le Soleil Noir*, a lost 1974 masterpiece, to lock it in his private vault forever.

## Scene 1. EXT. THE GRAND LUMIÈRE THEATER - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm)
Rain on cobblestones, neon in puddles. Vance in a queue of tuxedos and silk gowns under the glowing Art Deco marquee.
Jazz from inside. He slides a forged magnetic-stripe VIP card from his sleeve. Two spots ahead, a suited FLOOR
MANAGER steps out of the gilded doors, whispers to the BOUNCER and hands him a brushed-steel biometric palm scanner on
a thick coiled cable. The Bouncer bolts it onto the podium.
- BOUNCER: "Directives from Mr. Pendleton. Magnetic cards are void. Palm verification only."
Vance's composure flickers for a fraction of a second. The guest ahead presses a hand to the glass: BEEP, green, in.
Vance steps up.
- BOUNCER: "Hand on the glass."
SMASH CUT, clapperboard clack, raw camera: the bars snap away, the grain vanishes; the camera pans from the steps
across the wet street and pushes through the fogged windshield of an idling 1978 florist van.

## Scene 2. INT. FLORIST VAN - CONTINUOUS (Max, raw)
Max wedged between buckets of fake lilies and a humming rack of stolen telecom gear, sweating. On his main CRT, a live
security feed of Vance's hand hovering above the scanner.
- MAX: "Palm reader? Seriously?! Give me three seconds, Vance. Do not touch that glass yet!"
**Gameplay: signal-intercept minigame.** Tune two analog dials to match the scanner's oscillating frequency wave, and
patch a bypass cable, before Vance's hesitation looks suspicious. BZZT-LOCK: Max slams a toggle.
- MAX (grinning): "You're a French duke now. Drop the hand."
SMASH CUT, 35mm, Vance: palm flat on the scanner, a red laser sweeps his fingers, CHIME, green.
- BOUNCER (stepping aside): "Enjoy the screening, Your Grace."
Vance nods and glides inside.

## Scene 3. INT. GRAND FOYER & STAIRCASE - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm)
Gold leaf, crimson velvet, crystal chandeliers. On the marble staircase a tipsy FILM CRITIC stumbles down and sloshes
champagne on Vance's lapel.
- CRITIC: "Mon Dieu! Watch the... wait. I know that jawline. You're the financier from Zurich! Brilliant piece in
  Cahiers last month!"
**Gameplay: dialogue choice.**
- Play Along: "Keep your voice down, darling. I'm incognito." Gains the **VIP Alibi** perk (bypass one guard check).
- Brush Off: "You have the wrong man." The critic scoffs and wanders off; neutral heat.
Vance enters the MAIN HALL: waiters, socialites, a string quartet, guests by towering ice sculptures. At the far end,
velvet ropes and four armed PENDLETON GUARDS block the arched entrances to the Private Screening Auditorium, patrolling
tight loops, checking names on clipboards.
- MAX (V.O.): "Too many eyes on the velvet rope, Vance. You walk up there without a seat assignment, they'll tackle
  you into the shrimp cocktail. Nora, you in position?"
SMASH CUT, raw.

## Scene 4. INT. THE CATWALKS / LIGHTING RIG - CONTINUOUS (Nora, raw)
High above the chandelier Nora crawls along a narrow steel catwalk, sixty feet over the crowd. Through the grating:
a bird's-eye view of the whole hall, every guard's patrol cone and vision line.
- NORA: "I'm in the rafters. Looking down at the whole chessboard. Vance, hold by the bar. Let me clear your lane."
**Gameplay: tactical bird's-eye stealth.** From above, the player uses:
1. **Rigged spotlight:** loosen a clamp on an overhead stage light; the beam blinds the East Door guard.
2. **Champagne tower sabotage:** a weighted line from above taps the latch on a rolling service cart; a cascade of
   crystal glasses crashes near the West Pillar.
- GUARD 1 (shielding eyes): "What the hell, fix that spot! (to Guard 2) And go help the staff clean that mess before
  Pendleton sees it!"
SMASH CUT, 35mm: one unbroken tracking shot alongside Vance as he strolls past the distracted guards and slips behind
the crimson curtain into the dark auditorium as the doors swing shut.

## Scene 5. INT. PRIVATE AUDITORIUM - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm)
Plush velvet rows fill with billionaires and collectors. On stage, PENDLETON at a brass podium in a single spotlight.
- PENDLETON: "Tonight, you witness history. Le Soleil Noir. The only surviving print in existence. Tomorrow, it
  enters my private vault, never to be seen by common eyes again."
He gestures to the booth. House lights cut to pitch black. A beam erupts from the Projection Booth high at the back;
titles hit the screen; a thunderous orchestral score.
**Gameplay: cinematic stealth.** Vance slips out of his aisle seat and moves low along the shadowy side wall, timing
his steps to the booming timpani of the score, to the heavy oak door to the Backstage & Projection Stairwell: locked
with an electronic deadbolt.
- VANCE (into cuff): "Booth door is mag-locked from the inside. Cole, you're up."
SMASH CUT, raw.

## Scene 6. INT. BACKSTAGE CORRIDOR & RIGGING - CONTINUOUS (Cole, raw)
Tight, claustrophobic, dust and hot wiring. Cole behind wooden shipping crates behind the giant screen; through the
translucent screen, reversed ghostly images of the film flicker on his face. Two ENFORCERS patrol the narrow catwalk to
the Projection Booth.
- COLE: "Two heavies blocking the stairs. Going dark."
**Gameplay: stealth takedown,** using the film's loud gunfight audio as cover:
- Sneak behind Enforcer A during an on-screen explosion: silent choke-hold, drag him into a costume trunk.
- Pull a manual **counterweight lever**: a sandbag drops on a theatrical thunder-sheet, CRASH, luring Enforcer B down
  the stairs.
- Slip up the steps, knock out the lone projectionist, hit the **Mag-Lock Override** on the booth door.
- COLE: "Door's open. Make it quick."

## Scene 7. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm)
The hot, rattling booth; the 35mm projector hums. Vance opens the upper magazine, unthreads the master print of Le
Soleil Noir, swaps in a weighted dummy reel, latches the silver canister, tucks it under his arm. CLICK: the door
opens. PENDLETON with his CHIEF BODYGUARD; his eyes drop to the canister.
- PENDLETON: "Lock the building. Nobody leaves."
He slams the red manual fire alarm. Red strobes; the film burns out on the screen; guests scream for the exits.
**Gameplay: scripted cinematic fist fight,** three prompts, shot like a 1970s thriller:
1. **PARRY:** the bodyguard's heavy right hook; Vance ducks and slams his head into the hot projector housing; sparks,
   the dummy film catches fire.
2. **BLOCK WITH CANISTER:** Pendleton's concealed stiletto slashes; Vance raises the aluminium canister, CLANG, sparks.
3. **COUNTER / THROW:** Vance sweeps Pendleton's legs; he crashes back through the booth's viewing glass onto the
   velvet curtains below.
Cole bursts through the stairwell door and throws a smoke grenade down the steps at the incoming guards.
- COLE (over the alarm): "Max is out back! Move!"

## Scene 8. EXT. REAR ALLEYWAY - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm)
Vance and Cole kick open the steel fire exit into the rain-slick alley. Headlights: a sleek silver late-60s Italian GT
coupe drifts round the corner, passenger door already open; Max leans across from the driver's seat.
- MAX: "Get in! Get in!"
Armed guards burst from the fire exit; muzzle flashes; bullets spark off the brick. Vance dives into the passenger seat
clutching the canister; Cole leaps into the back; Max dumps the clutch; the V8 roars, fishtailing into the rainy
Parisian night.
**Final shot:** in the car, Vance checks his reflection in the rear-view mirror, straightens his tie, and smiles.
- VANCE: "Cut. Print it."
SMASH TO BLACK. End credits roll over film reel sprockets.
