TITLE: DIRECTOR'S CUT SETTING: The Aurelia Auction House — One continuous, interconnected 3D soundstage (Entrance Lobby, Main Exhibition Hall, Upper AV Catwalk, and Backstage Service Corridor). CORE RULE: Julian is always "On Camera." Every move he makes is tracked by live broadcast drones, security cameras, and AI-driven guards. If he breaks character, stops moving, or fumbles a conversation, his Composure Meter drains and security swarms him. You must cut to Maya (Upper Catwalk) and Vex (Backstage) in real time—using a 0.5-second slow-motion landing buffer—to manipulate the physical set so Julian can pull off the heist in a single, seamless take. If he gets caught, a Blooper Slate rewinds the scene in two seconds flat.

SCENE 1: THE BAIT-AND-SWITCH (The Hook & First Cut)

EXT./INT. AURELIA GALA LOBBY - NIGHT

A velvet-roped line of tuxedos and evening gowns snakes toward a reinforced security checkpoint. Warm golden chandeliers reflect off polished marble.

JULIAN (30s, tailored tuxedo, effortless charm) stands four people back from the metal detector. Inside his cuff, a cloned RFID VIP pass glows faintly green.

Two spots ahead, a broad-shouldered SECURITY CHIEF steps up to the podium with a rugged tablet and barks into his earpiece.

SECURITY CHIEF: Unplug the RFID readers. New directive from the board—iris biometric match against the guest registry. No scan, no entry.

The guards yank the RFID cables. A blue laser scanner clicks onto the podium.

Julian's confident smirk vanishes. His eyes dart to the exit—blocked by two armed guards behind him.

JULIAN: (under his breath) Shit.

GAMEPLAY INTERVENTION — THE FIRST CUT: The frame freezes in high-contrast black-and-white. A Director's Clapperboard UI snaps across the screen (TAKE 01 // SCENE: THE ENTRANCE). The camera rips backward out of Julian's point of view, soaring upward through the lobby rafters and dropping into a 3-way GTA-style Character Wheel:

* JULIAN (On-Camera / Lead — Composure: 100%)
* MAYA (Off-Camera / AV Catwalk)
* VEX (Off-Camera / Backstage)

You flick the wheel to MAYA.

INT. UPPER AV CATWALK - CONTINUOUS

The camera swoops through the rafters, triggering a 0.5-second slow-motion landing buffer as it locks behind MAYA (20s, stagehand rig, headset, glowing amber outline on the Zone A Breaker). Below her, through the steel grating, Julian is now two people away from the iris scanner. A 15-second Composure Timer ticks down over Julian's head.

* Player Action: Maya pulls the breaker for Zone A (Lobby Spotlights) and cranks the manual spotlight rig directly onto the Security Chief's biometric tablet.

INT. AURELIA GALA LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Down below, the overhead chandeliers snap off. Simultaneously, a blinding 5,000-watt stage spotlight slams onto the security podium, washing out the iris scanner's optical sensor in pure white glare.

GUARD: Chief, the optical sensor's blinded! It's throwing a hardware fault!
SECURITY CHIEF: (shielding his eyes) Keep the line moving! Check the paper manifest and wave them through!

Switch back to JULIAN. The camera drops back to floor level. Julian steps up to the podium, flashes a million-dollar smile, and taps his name on the clipboard. The guard waves him into the main hall.

SCENE 2: THE EXHIBITION FLOOR (Live AI Improvisation & Sabotage)

INT. MAIN EXHIBITION HALL - CONTINUOUS

Julian steps onto the main floor. In the center of the room sits a rotating glass pedestal holding the Chronos Ledger—an antique brass-bound tome. Flanking the pedestal is DIRECTOR VANCE, the gala's host, wearing the Master Pedestal Keycard clipped to his belt, guarded by two stationary BODYGUARDS.

Overhead, a live Broadcast Drone orbits the room, keeping a red tally light trained on Julian's sector.

GAMEPLAY LOOP — STAYING IN CHARACTER: While controlling Julian, you cannot crouch, sprint, or interact with restricted doors. You walk along the gilded carpet, hit contextual [BLEND IN] prompts (sipping champagne, admiring paintings) to maintain Composure, and maneuver toward the dais.

Obstacle 1: The Laser Cordon & AI Patrol Guard

A velvet rope with an active infrared tripwire blocks the steps to the dais. A PATROL GUARD steps into Julian's path, suspiciously eyeing his guest wristband.

* Live AI Interrogation Mechanic: A dynamic dialogue prompt triggers. The guard asks an unscripted question ("I don't recognize your face from the donor list—who invited you?"). You pick a smooth bluff ("Lord Sterling's private office—check the late-addition wire"), buying yourself a 12-second stall window while the guard radios dispatch to verify.
* Cut to VEX (Backstage Service Corridor): The camera punches through a faux-marble wall into the cramped backstage hallway. VEX (30s, catering vest) lands in a 0.5-second slow-motion frame facing a highlighted Rolling Champagne Cart beside an unlocked service door.
* Player Action: Vex unlatches the service door and kicks the wheel chock out from under the loaded champagne tower cart. It rolls onto the marble floor and shatters ten feet to the left of the dais.
* Cut to JULIAN: The Patrol Guard abandons the radio check, curses, and rushes toward the broken glass. Julian casually steps over the unguarded velvet rope—stopping inches from the live infrared tripwire.
* Cut to MAYA (AV Catwalk): From above, Maya triggers the Gala Banner Drop, releasing a massive silk tapestry directly across the infrared beam—creating a physical shield over the sensor while blocking the Broadcast Drone's line of sight for 8 seconds.

Obstacle 2: Lifting the Master Keycard

Julian reaches the dais, standing three feet behind Director Vance. Vance is facing the crowd, flanked by the two Bodyguards.

* Cut to MAYA: Maya swings the main stage spotlight rig directly onto Director Vance, triggering the gala's Podium Audio Cue (an orchestral fanfare swell).
* Cut to VEX: Backstage, Vex yanks the main teleprompter/audio patch cable out of the wall socket and plugs in a high-pitched feedback loop.
* Result in the Main Hall: Director Vance leans into the podium mic—and gets hit with a piercing screech of audio feedback. Both Bodyguards instinctively turn their heads toward the podium and cover their ears.
* Cut to JULIAN: Julian steps neatly behind Vance, mimics reaching out to "adjust" the malfunctioning mic cable on Vance's lapel, and smoothly unclips the Master Pedestal Keycard from Vance's belt. His Composure Meter surges to MAX.

(Note — The Blooper Failsafe: If at any point Julian's Composure hits 0%, the screen freezes with a loud buzzer and a red slate: "CUT! BLOOPER — JULIAN CAUGHT RED-HANDED." It holds for 1.5 seconds on Julian awkwardly holding the keycard while guards tackle him, then seamlessly rewinds to the start of the current obstacle.)

SCENE 3: THE CLIMAX — "ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE"

INT. MAIN EXHIBITION HALL - STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Julian steps up to the glass pedestal and slides the keycard into the slot. The reinforced glass casing hisses downward into the floor.

The moment Julian's gloved fingers close around the Chronos Ledger, a silent perimeter alarm triggers. Red strobe lights pulse along the baseboards. Heavy steel lockdown shutters begin descending over every public exit.

FINAL SEQUENCE — RAPID CROSS-CUTTING (15-SECOND COUNTDOWN):

1. ON JULIAN: The two Bodyguards spin around, spotting Julian holding the ledger. The Broadcast Drone locks its red laser onto Julian's chest. Security teams sprint toward the stage from both flanks. Julian cannot run—if he breaks his stride, he's tackled.
2. CUT TO MAYA (Catwalk): You snap up to the rafters. Maya grabs the Pyrotechnic Finale Switch—meant for the end-of-night auction reveal—and slams it down.
   * Effect: Four stage-edge spark fountains erupt in a blinding wall of golden sparks and thick theatrical smoke between the Bodyguards and Julian, blinding the Broadcast Drone's lens.
3. CUT TO VEX (Backstage): You whip behind the stage curtain. Vex grabs the Counterweight Winch Lever and kicks the brake release.
   * Effect: A massive velvet stage backdrop drops from the rafters directly behind Julian, physically walling off the stage from the approaching security guards, while a hidden backstage escape hatch swings open beneath the podium.
4. FINAL CUT TO JULIAN: You snap back to the floor for the final 3 seconds. Surrounded by golden sparks and billowing smoke, Julian tucks the Chronos Ledger neatly under his arm, turns directly toward the spinning, blinded Broadcast Drone, and offers a slow, theatrical bow.

Just as the heavy velvet curtain hits the stage floor and the security guards slam into the other side—

SNAP TO BLACK. A crisp white Director's Clapperboard slams shut in the center of the screen:

SCENE 01 — PRINT IT. CONTINUITY: 100% // BLOOPERS: 0 // RATING: 10/10
