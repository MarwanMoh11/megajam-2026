# Scene 2: the florist van, Max on the dials (s2-van)

Code: `Reel/Source/Reel/Take/Script/Scene2Van.h/.cpp` (`AScene2Van`, SceneNumber 2, POV Max, `bPlayerWalks=false`).
Max has no body to drive: the actor is its own camera (`SceneCamera = this`). Level overlays, both re-runnable, under
the editor lock: `tools/script/scene2_van.py` (the set, tag `script_s2_set`) then `tools/script/scene2.py` (the scene
actor `Script_S2_Van`, tag `script_s2`, its camera points and 12 sounds). Test: `Reel.Script.Jump 2`;
PIE shots: `tools/script/scene2_pie.py [prefix] | --fail | --stop` (into `C:\Jam\Builds\script\s2\`).

## Beats
1. Opening (5 s, SPACE skips): the camera pans from the theater steps (Vance at the palm plate) across the wet
   street to the van (FLEURS DU SOIR), pushes through the fogged windshield and settles on Max at his CRT.
2. Max: "Palm reader? Seriously?!..." The minigame: the main CRT (left) is a live SceneCapture feed of the podium;
   the scope (right) shows the scanner's drifting amber wave and Max's green one. A/D turn frequency, LEFT/RIGHT
   turn gain; while both are in the window SYNC fills (0.7 s). SPACE patches the bypass.
3. SPACE before SYNC is full: a spark, "Not yet, not yet! Sync first!", +12% hesitation. Vance's hesitation bar fills
   over 24 s (+5 s a retake, up to 3); the bouncer speaks at 45% and 75%; full = `FailScene("Vance_hesitated_at_the_glass")`.
   A retake skips the opening ("Again. Three seconds, Vance...").
4. Lock: BZZT-LOCK stamp, the toggle, "You're a French duke now. Drop the hand." After 2.6 s, `CutTo(Vance)` and
   Scene 1's `PalmAccepted()` (called by name on `AScene1Marquee`: green light, gate, "Enjoy the screening, Your Grace.").
5. `FinishScene()` when Scene 1 sets flag `S1PalmThrough` (9 s safety timeout): the cut to Scene 3.

## Test hooks
