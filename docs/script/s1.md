# Scene 1: EXT. THE GRAND LUMIÈRE THEATER - NIGHT (s1-marquee)

- **Class:** `AScene1Marquee` (`Reel/Source/Reel/Take/Script/Scene1Marquee.*`). The beat sheet is at the top of the header.
  The actor is the palm scanner's podium at the door. `tools/script/scene1.py` (tag `script_s1`) places it at
  (-3610, 255), yaw 90, and tunes the queue: scan 1.8 s, and the Bouncer moved behind the podium to (-3625, 180).
- **Reuse:** `AQueueLine` runs the line in script mode (`ScriptReset`, `ScriptStep`, `bScriptHoldFront`,
  `bScriptOpenFront`). The old beats (the refusal, Maya's taught switch, the searchlight, the lobby) are off whenever
  a script sequencer runs. The queue's doorman is the BOUNCER. `AScannerWatcher::ScriptOpen` holds the arch open and
  turns it green.
- **Scene 2 hooks:** `StageAtPlate()` (Scene 2's start state), `PalmAccepted()` (the laser, the chime, green, "Enjoy
  the screening, Your Grace", the gate opens), `IsPalmDone()`, `PalmSeconds()`, `GetPlatePoint()`.
- **Dressing:** `tools/script/scene1_dress.py` (tag `script_s1_dress`) does the Art Deco marquee THE GRAND LUMIÈRE,
  the rain, wet cobbles and neon.
- **Audio:** `/Game/Take/Audio/Script/S1/` holds the voices for the floor manager and the bouncer, plus the beep, the
  laser and the rain loop. Missing sounds are skipped and the subtitles still show. The jazz is `mus_bed`, low-passed
  at 700 Hz.
- **Retake:** no fail state of its own. Pushing the line gets the queue's existing nudges.
