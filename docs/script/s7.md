# Scene 7: from the rigged door to the fight (s7-route)

Marwan's QA (Nov 15 evening): "The party system doesn't make sense. Add a narratively sensical sequence where I
actually enter from the door I wanted unlocked and go to the film room, steal the film, then cut to the venue: the
film stops and the alarm sounds, and then we meet in a room with the guard and the owner for a fighting sequence."

## What "the party system" was, and the fix
The crew's hand-offs didn't connect in space. Vance waited at the oak door in the auditorium, Cole opened a different
"booth door" up a stair behind the screen, and the next cut found Vance already inside a projection box over the lobby
doors, which he had never walked to (its stairs floated in the hall). Now the hand-offs are physical: Cole rigs the
scanner of the door Vance is standing at, opens it from the backstage side, tells him the way and goes to ready the
exit (the prop store, whose door s6 wedged open). Nora (rafters) and Max (cameras) talk him along the route and through the escape. Pendleton and his
bodyguard reach him by the grand stair, the way they'd really come from the auditorium.

## The set (`tools/script/scene7.py`, tag `script_s7`)
- **cut**: the hall's south wall opened at the oak door (x 3580..3720, 260 high); the wall piece there is hidden with
  no collision (tag `s7_cut`) and rebuilt around the opening. Scene 5's slab and its fittings are tagged `s7_oak_slab`:
  `AScene7Route` hides them and swings its own leaf (`DoorLeaf`, in s5's oak material) while part 0 runs.
- **shell**: the booth (x -1000..-600, y -200..800, floor 450) and the **projectionist's stair**: 24 solid steps from
  the south landing down to the hall floor, x -760..-600, walls, roof, two lamps, a door at the foot (y -1140) with a
  PROJECTION sign. It replaces the six-step stub that floated over the hall entrance.
- **hide**: the old box's stairs, landing, front wall and ports are hidden for good (no collision). Scene 5's
  Pendleton ("Vance") and bodyguard are hidden only while part 1 runs.
- **scene**: `S7_Route` + `S7_Route_StartSpot` (3650, -1900, facing the door) and `S7_Booth` (tag `Part=2`) +
  `S7_StartSpot` on the projector mark (-860, 150, 450) facing +X.
