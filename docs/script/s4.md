# Scene 4: the catwalks (s4-catwalk)

INT. THE CATWALKS / LIGHTING RIG - CONTINUOUS. Nora's raw feed, then the 35mm tracking shot of Vance.

## Files
- `Reel/Source/Reel/Take/Script/Scene4Catwalk.h/.cpp`: `AScene4Catwalk` (SceneNumber 4, POV Nora).
- `FollowSpot.*` (`ResetSpot`, `HoldBeam`) and `CartDevice.*` (`ResetCart`): the two devices, reset for retakes.
- `tools/script/scene4.py`: the overlay (tag `script_s4`). Run it after `scene3.py`, because the guards are Scene 3's.
- `tools/script/scene4_pie.py [fail|--stop]`: the play test from `Reel.Script.Jump 4` (shots in `C:\Jam\Builds\script\s4\`),
  pressing through the console hook `Reel.S4.Press 1|2|cue`.
- `tools/script/scene4_audio.py`: the voice lines (ElevenLabs, voices Lily = Nora/Maya and Chris = guards).
  Files go to `C:\Jam\Audio\script\s4\` and are imported into `/Game/Take/Audio/Script/S4/`.

## How it plays
- Opens on Nora's bird's-eye camera (`S4_BirdsEyeCam`, over the platform rail at z 1240), with a slow vertigo sway.
  Nora says her line. The HUD draws the gold lane (the bar to the rope gap) and each guard's cone: red while it
  covers the lane, grey while he is distracted, green for Guard 2 with the VIP Alibi.
- **[1] Spot clamp:** the follow spot fires at the EAST DOOR guard (S3_Guard_NorthGap), who is blinded and turns
  away, for `SpotSeconds` (7 s). GUARD 1 (S3_Guard_SouthGap) turns to yell at it while it burns.
- **[2] Cart latch:** `S4_ChampagneCart` rolls from the south wall to the West Pillar (column 2200, -1300) in 1.6 s.
  Its tower of 30 glasses flies and shatters (`sfx_glass_smash`). The WEST PILLAR guard (S3_Guard_SouthArch) walks
  to the mess.
  - If the spot is burning, Guard 1 delivers the script's line and sends GUARD 2 (S3_Guard_NorthArch) to the mess.
  - If the spot is not burning and Vance has no alibi, Guard 1 shouts "Hold your posts!": the guards close ranks,
    and a retake follows ("THE GUARDS CLOSED RANKS").
- **[SPACE] Cue Vance:** if any cone is red, a guard calls out and a retake follows ("SPOTTED AT THE ROPE").
  Otherwise Nora says "Lane's clear" and the scene cuts to Vance.
- **The tracking shot:** `S4_TrackCam` rides beside Vance, a pace ahead, as the scene carries him along the lane
  (bar, west of the seated benches, the centre aisle) at `StrollSpeed` past the guards
  (the spot keeps burning). It holds at the curtain while he walks into the dark aisle (2720, 0), and the walnut
  doors swing shut. Then `FinishScene`.
- VipAlibi: read, not required. It excuses Guard 2, so the crash-first order works too. It is consumed on success.
- The scene moves its guards itself (`bScriptHeld`, and a late tick after the director's watcher step) and puts them back on
  `EndScene`. The cart's `Range` is 1, so the director lures nobody with it.
