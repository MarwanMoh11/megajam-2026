## What plays
1. Speech: the house low, Pendleton alone in a hard spot at the brass podium; Vance stands at his aisle seat (south
   end of a reserved row, (2650,-690)) and cannot walk. Space/Enter skips. A retake starts at the blackout.
2. Pendleton points at the booth; the house lights CUT to black, the projector's beam, the film, LE SOLEIL NOIR on the
   screen, the score and the first timpani hit.
3. Sneak: a timpani hit every 1.6 s flares the screen and the HUD's drum mark. Two torches sweep the south floor (an
   usher at the front, a guard at the rope's south end). In a torch beam, moving outside the boom's window fills SEEN;
   full = the blooper SEEN IN THE DARK (VipAlibi from Scene 3 saves him once: "Pardon me, sir").
4. The oak door on the south wall (x 3650): the deadbolt buzzes red, VANCE into the cuff, FinishScene -> Cole.
