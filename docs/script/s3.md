## Beats
1. Vance at `S3_StartSpot` (-3020, 220), just in from the doors. The FILM CRITIC (`S3_Critic`, plum tailcoat, a coupe,
   tag `NoSetLife` so set life never casts him) stands four steps up the grand stair (-2420, 90, z 74).
2. After 1.2 s (or when Vance comes within 4.5 m) he stumbles down at Vance, swaying; the camera eases to him.
3. He reaches Vance (or 3.5 s pass): champagne splash (droplets + a wet mark on the lapel), Vance is held, his line.
4. The choice (1 / 2, or arrows + E/Enter/Space): **Play Along** sets flag `VipAlibi` (a gold "VIP ALIBI" tag shows
   top-left while it is unspent) and his reply; **Brush Off**: he scoffs and wanders off. Vance walks again at once.
5. The main hall: past x 1300 Max's V.O.; 7.5 s later `FinishScene()` (the cut to Nora, Scene 4).
6. The rope: past x 2130 the nearest rope guard stops him. With `VipAlibi` it is spent (the guard sends him back to the
   bar); without it he is tackled: `FailScene("Tackled")`, retake from step 1 (the flag is cleared on a retake).
