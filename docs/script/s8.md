# Scene 8. The escape and the getaway (Vance, 35mm): notes (owner s8-escape, was s8-alley)

## What is built (Nov 15 evening rework: Marwan's QA "no teleports, make them actually escape, running, a chase")
- `AScene8Alley` (`Reel/Source/Reel/Take/Script/Scene8Alley.*`), SceneNumber 8, placed by `tools/script/scene8.py`
  (tag `script_s8`) on the outside face of the steel fire exit in the prop store's south wall (x 1300, y -3322, street
  z -21). scene8.py cuts a real opening for that door in `Service_South` (hidden + collision off, rebuilt round the
  opening as `S8_Wall_*`), adds an EXIT sign and green glow inside, and places `S8_StartSpot` on Scene 7's fight mark.
- **Escape** (Vance's follow camera, `bAllowRun`, Vance forced to run): opens in the security room where the fight
  ended (-2650, -1680, z 450, yaw 90). Cole kicks in through the east door, lobs a smoke grenade out of the north
  balcony door (`M_S7_Smoke` puffs), "Max is out back! Move!", and leads along `RouteWorld` (scene8.py `ROUTE`): the
  south fire stair down, the lobby, the hall's west end, the staff door (x -300), east down the backstage corridor,
  the prop store door (1700, -2600), between the crates to the fire exit. Cole waits (turned to Vance) whenever Vance
  is more than `ColeLeadMax` behind: he is the guidance.
- **The chase:** three `AScene8Chaser` guards (new, `Scene8Chaser.*`: SKM_NPC_Guard jogging with a pistol, SM_Pistol
  in hand_r; grey box if the mesh is missing) appear at the foot of the fire stair once Vance is `GapStart` past it
  and run Vance's own recorded trail (stairs and doors included, no navigation). The lead guard holds a gap that
  closes from `GapStart` 10 m to `GapEnd` 6 m; they fire every 0.28-0.6 s: muzzle flash + light, a tracer, the round
  traced into the set beside him (`MissStart` 2.2 m -> `MissEnd` 0.7 m) with sparks, light and a ricochet where it
  lands; a guard with a wall in the way holds fire. Never a hit. **The stumble** (corridor, about x 500): a round
  at his shoulder, a grunt, he drops to a walk for 0.7 s and the gap visibly closes. **Standing still**: they close to
  `MinGap` 3.2 m, Cole shouts "Don't stop! Move!", KEEP MOVING on the HUD, and after `StallSeconds` 5 s there:
  `FailScene("Caught")` (a retake from the security room).
- **Alley:** the door bangs open as Cole or Vance reaches its bar; the car's run in is cued when Vance is 13 m from
  the exit, MAX "Get in! Get in!"; the guards spill out of the exit to their marks and fire. Reach the passenger door
  within `AlleySeconds` 9 s, else `FailScene("Pinned_Down")`. Cole makes for the back seat.
- **Dive / Drive / Mirror:** unchanged from s8-alley (the car-door hand-off to s8-car-mirror is proposed: they own the
  in-car shots and the car).
- Test autopilot: console `Reel.S8.Autopilot 1` makes Vance run the route to the car door himself
  (`tools/script/scene8_pie.py` uses it). WASD takes over.
- Tunables are `UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere)` on the actor (`Scene8|Escape`, `Run`, `Timing`, `Car`, `Cameras`, `Assets`).

## Hand-off to Spine (the end)
Scene 8 plays "Cut. Print it." itself, then calls `FinishScene()`. Spine's `EndOfFilm` then goes straight to SMASH
TO BLACK and the credits over sprockets (no second "Cut. Print it." over it).
