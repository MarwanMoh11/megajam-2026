# Scene 8. EXT. REAR ALLEYWAY - NIGHT (Vance, 35mm): notes (owner s8-alley)

## What is built
- `AScene8Alley` (`Reel/Source/Reel/Take/Script/Scene8Alley.*`), SceneNumber 8, placed by `tools/script/scene8.py`
  (tag `script_s8`) on the outside face of a steel fire exit in the service wing's south wall (x 1300, y -3322, on the
  street at z -21). The scene owns the door, two guards, the car (`AScene8Car`, `Scene8Car.*`), its three cameras and
  the start spot (spawned at BeginPlay); the overlay owns the alley set (far brick wall along y -4100 with a gap at
  x 2300..2900, brick facing on the building, bins, crates, puddles, lamps) and hides the street trees inside it.
- Beats: **Burst** (wide shot: the door bangs open, Vance and Cole stumble out, the GT drifts in round the west corner,
  headlights flare, passenger door swings open; MAX "Get in! Get in!") -> **Run** (Vance's follow camera, the player
  walks: reach the passenger door within 7 s; guards burst out at 0.6 s, shots every 0.3-0.6 s spark off the brick,
  closing from 3.2 m to 0.7 m; Cole runs for the back seat on his own; timeout = `FailScene("PinnedDown")`) ->
  **Dive** (door slams; COLE "Go, go, go!") -> **Drive** (camera at the alley's west end tracks the car as it
  fishtails out through the gap) -> **Mirror** (final shot framed in the rear-view mirror: Vance straightens his tie;
  VANCE "Cut. Print it.") -> `FinishScene()`.
- Tunables are `UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere)` on the actor (`Scene8|Timing`, `Run`, `Car`, `Cameras`, `Assets`).

## Hand-off to Spine (the end)
Scene 8 plays "Cut. Print it." itself, then calls `FinishScene()`. Spine's `EndOfFilm` then goes straight to SMASH
TO BLACK and the credits over sprockets (no second "Cut. Print it." over it).
